# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, focus discussion on the work, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, or personally identifying content.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Use GitHub's built-in reporting and moderation tools for conduct concerns. Do
not post private or personally identifying details in a public issue.
